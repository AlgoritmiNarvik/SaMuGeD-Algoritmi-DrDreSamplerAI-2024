Render metadata

These are MIDI instrument renders, not extracts from commercial recordings. Notes, velocities, tempo and the MIDI instrument program come from the source arrangement. A measured or meter padded recurrence cycle includes the selected source part across that cycle, which can extend beyond the detector excerpt. Reverb and note releases are carried into the next cycle through repeated rendering.

Familiar song evidence comes from published recognition or involuntary musical imagery studies. It supports the song labels, not recognition of these exact MIDI fragments. The selection is a documented curation, not a new listener ranking. The other collections rank structural recurrence in saved MIDI arrangements. Search caps and source naming errors can affect the results. No listener ratings were collected.
