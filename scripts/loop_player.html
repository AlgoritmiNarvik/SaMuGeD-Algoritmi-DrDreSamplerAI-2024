40 playable loops · 95,077 dataset phrases · 48 kHz stereo audio

MIDI instrument renders preserve source notes, velocities, tempo and programs. Measured or meter padded cycles may extend beyond the detector excerpt. Repeated rendering carries reverb and note releases across the loop boundary. These are not commercial recordings.

Published recognition and involuntary musical imagery studies support familiar song labels, not these exact fragments. Selections combine documented curation and MIDI recurrence. Search limits and source naming errors affect results. No listener ratings were collected.
