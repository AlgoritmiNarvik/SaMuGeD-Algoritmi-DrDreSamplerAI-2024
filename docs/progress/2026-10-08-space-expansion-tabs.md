# 2026-10-08 PDMX and MAESTRO tabs in the listening Space

The Space now has two more tabs, PDMX scores and MAESTRO performances, with fifty loops each. The phrases are the most repeated melodic phrases of the published expansion configurations under the Lakh motif filter, one per title, ranked by occurrence count inside the score or performance. They were rendered from the local source MIDI with the existing loop renderer, one run per PDMX batch build, so each cycle carries the source notes of its part, and re-rendered with the ColomboGMGS2 bank and the Original effect profile of the current Space.

`scripts/extend_loop_space.py` holds the three steps: `select` ranks one corpus and writes a selection file, `build` binds the renders to a copy of the live Space, appends the groups, regenerates the page and the card and writes `rendering/expansion_tabs.json`, and `pin` rewrites the audio base URL after the audio upload. The player template reads a `group_order` from the catalog, because the page sorted groups by key and the new tabs landed between the old ones, and a `tempo_label` per row, so MAESTRO shows its nominal tempo as "no tempo map". `scripts/publish_huggingface.py` gained `--space-only`, which adds or replaces files in the existing Space without deleting anything.

Published state: audio repository commit `0dd8e427` with 100 FLAC files, Space commit `517b8b29` with 703 added or changed files (MIDI, metadata, note views, catalog, page, card and receipts). Nothing was removed from either repository and the Lakh loops are untouched.
