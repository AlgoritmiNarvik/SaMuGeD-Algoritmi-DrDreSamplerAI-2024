# 2026-10-08 Duplicate MusicBrainz works count as one work for MAESTRO

After the catalogue number matching, 122 MAESTRO sources still landed in the `multiple_works` tier. Almost all of them were one composition listed more than once in MusicBrainz, either as revisions linked by `revision of` (three entries for Rachmaninoff's Sonata op. 36) or as plain duplicates with the same catalogue number and a different spelling (`Études, Op. 25: No. 10` beside `Étude op. 25 no. 10 "Octaves"`). Two things close the gap.

The catalogue index (`work-candidates-v5-catalogue`) names the MusicBrainz link types correctly. The former `revision` never matched the real `revision of`, so a revised work and its original both stayed as candidates. Rebuilt as `work_identity_offline_catalogue_v02.sqlite` from the same subset: 946 of the 1,011 candidate sources now have a single work, up from 888.

The assessment (`candidate-assessment-v3`) forms a duplicate family from the catalogue candidates of one source that share the composer and a specific catalogue key, or a bare opus together with an equal normalized title. The family counts as its lowest work id. Every row of the source records the members as `work_family`, the other members and the rows of other providers record the representative as `canonical_work` and no row of the source reaches the full tier. Nickname matches never form a family, and numbered pieces of one opus keep their own titles and stay separate. The merge (`work-identity-merge-v3`) exports the representative as `counts_as` on each candidate.

Assessment v04, export v08 and publication folder v03 follow. MAESTRO now has 561 sources in full agreement, 439 at the weaker tier and 28 with several works (nickname matches such as "La Campanella" and bare opus numbers that cover different works). Lakh and PDMX are unchanged. The card coverage table, the consumer guide and the research docs describe the rule. The `work_identity` Parquet files, the card and the guide were uploaded to `AlmazErmilov/samuged-recurring-phrases` as an add or replace commit. Nothing here is a verified identity or a rights clearance.
