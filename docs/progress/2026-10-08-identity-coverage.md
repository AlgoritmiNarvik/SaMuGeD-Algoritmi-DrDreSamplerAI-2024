# 2026-10-08 Identity coverage for Lakh surnames and MAESTRO catalogue numbers

Two gaps of the work identity layer are closed offline. Lakh creators written as a bare surname (Battisti, Dalla, McCartney) never agreed with the full artist credit, and MAESTRO titles name a composer and a catalogue number that the title lookups could not read. Both corpora now have a candidate for about four sources in five, up from 78.8 and 6.9 percent.

The recordings index moves to policy `work-candidates-v4-fullexport`: a single artist credit also agrees when every token of the source creator (four letters or more) appears in its name, recorded as the weaker `surname_subset` agreement and never promoted to the full tier. The rebuilt index `work_identity_offline_recordings_v02.sqlite` has 14,288 candidate sources, 893 more than before.

`samuged/work_identity_offline_catalogue.py` is a new two step module. `subset` streams the verified MusicBrainz full export once more and keeps the works of the 59 composers named in the MAESTRO queue (39 by MBID through the offline creator identities, the rest by an unambiguous person name) with composer relations, aliases, attributes and work to work links. `prepare` matches every MAESTRO row through the composer and the catalogue number of the title (opus with number, BWV, K., D., Hob., WoO, S., L. and similar systems, gated by composer), or a quoted nickname when no number resolves. MusicBrainz keeps catalogue numbers in work titles and aliases, not in attributes, so the title path does the work and the attribute path stays for completeness. Matched works are reduced to the works themselves: highest matched ancestor, no arrangement of a matched work, no movement of a matched work and, for a bare opus, the set rather than its numbered pieces. The evidence records key and form agreement between the title and the work and the ids of the works reduced away. The index `work_identity_offline_catalogue_v01.sqlite` has candidates for 1,011 of 1,276 sources, 888 of them with a single work.

The assessment (`candidate-assessment-v2`) takes the catalogue index as a fourth provider, treats a specific catalogue number in the work title as full title agreement and a composer identity as full creator agreement, lowers the tier on a key or form disagreement, a bare opus, a nickname, a single namesake composer or a surname only agreement, and counts a candidate listed among the related parts of a catalogue candidate as that work, so a dump candidate for the first movement no longer conflicts with the catalogue candidate for the sonata. The merge (`work-identity-merge-v2`) adds `dump_catalogue_status` and `dump_catalogue_reason` to the row schema and the publication adds both columns to the `work_identity` configuration.

Assessment v03, export v07 and publication folder v02 were built from these indexes. MAESTRO has 1,028 sources with a candidate (557 single work full agreement, 349 weaker, 122 several works, no conflicts), Lakh 14,441 (unchanged 6,869 full agreement, 971 several works, 165 conflicts, all of them MusicBrainz duplicate works or mislabelled Lakh duplicates as reviewed earlier). The dataset card coverage table, the consumer guide and the citation (version 0.3) follow. The `work_identity` Parquet files, the card and the guide were uploaded to `AlmazErmilov/samuged-recurring-phrases` as commit `b594a1ef` (add or replace only, the phrase files were byte identical and stayed as they were). Nothing here is a verified identity or a rights clearance.

The listening Space gets a `tempo` step in `scripts/extend_loop_space.py`. For groups without a tempo map it estimates a pulse from the onset intervals of each loop (coverage of inter onset intervals by integer multiples and halves of a candidate period, longest qualifying period, folded into 60 to 180 BPM) and shows it instead of the nominal 120 of the MAESTRO files, labelled as an estimate that is ambiguous by a factor of two. On the 50 PDMX loops with a notated tempo the estimate is exact within 5 percent for 47 and within an octave for 49. The MAESTRO tab of the Space was updated as commit `552468f2` (catalog, page, card, per loop metadata and the receipt `rendering/tempo_estimates.json`).
